# Scoring fairness report

Generated by `scripts/fairness-report.ts` from 12 simulated Majors (1272 series, run through the real scoring code).
The players and results are fictional, so this tests the structure of the scoring model (formats, Swiss records, captain, team results), not real-world calibration. Re-run it on licensed historical data before launch (docs/08).

Model under test: `cs2-v2` (docs/04 Model C with neutral format weights). Per-map events, overtime normalised to 24 rounds, map win +3, maps averaged per series, series win +3, advancement credit for Swiss series a team didn't need to play, captain 1.5×.

## 1. Best-of-one vs best-of-three vs best-of-five

Average points per player per series. A fair model keeps these close: longer series shouldn't be worth more just for being longer.

| Model | BO1 | BO3 | BO5 | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cs2-v2` (maps averaged) | 25.1 | 24.9 | 24.8 | 1.2% |
| `cs2-v1` (format weights 0.8 / 1 / 1.2) | 20.1 | 24.9 | 29.7 | 38.8% |
| Raw sum of maps (no averaging) | 25.1 | 59.9 | 82.9 | 103.3% |

**Finding:** averaging maps keeps formats within a few percent of each other. Format weights (v1) and raw sums both reintroduce a large gap, which is why v2 is the default.

## 2. Swiss: 3–0 vs 3–1 vs 3–2

Average stage points for players on teams that advanced, by record. Without a correction, a 3–2 team plays two more series than a 3–0 team and earns more for it.

| Model | 3–0 | 3–1 | 3–2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| v2 without advancement credit | 95.6 | 114.7 | 130.8 |
| **v2 with advancement credit** | 145.6 | 139.7 | 130.8 |
| Raw sum, no credit | 160.3 | 198.2 | 246.5 |

**Finding:** with advancement credit, 3–0 players score 11.3% relative to 3–2 players (docs/04 wants "the same or slightly more" for the cleaner run). Without it, the 3–2 path is worth 36.8% more, which would reward needing more matches.

## 3. Team results vs individual play

Share of all points that come from map and series wins: **12.0%**. Individual events (kills, deaths, assists, opening kills, multi-kills, clutches) make up the rest, so a strong player on a losing team still scores, and winning still matters.

## 4. Captain

For random five-player lineups, the captain's extra 0.5× is **8.9%** of the lineup's total with a random captain, and **14.9%** with the best possible captain. It matters, but it can't outweigh the other four picks.

## 5. Determinism

Scoring every simulated tournament twice gives byte-identical results: **yes**. Production adds the ledger guarantees: input-hash idempotency, append-only corrections and pinned rule-set versions (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## 6. Live scoring under map averaging

If a map in progress counted toward the series average immediately, a player's running score would visibly fall when the next map started in **74.5%** of cases (map 1 final vs map 2 at half time, events only). That is why live maps are shown as provisional *map points* next to each player, and totals update when each map ends (docs/LIVE_DATA.md). Making totals tick with every kill would need a sum-based model, which section 1 shows is unfair across formats. This trade-off is open question G-1 in docs/GAMEPLAY_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
