## 1. HLTV Fantasy

**Why it matters:** HLTV is the default CS news and stats site, and its fantasy game is the incumbent we must beat for CS2.

### What we know about how it works

| Area | Finding | Label |
|------|---------|-------|
| Contest structure | One fantasy game per event, and often per stage. The EWC 2026 group stage (32 teams, 160 players) is one game; its playoffs are a separate game. | [V] [HLTV, EWC fantasy](https://www.hltv.org/news/45277/10000-fantasy-season-continues-with-32-team-ewc-group-stage) |
| Seasons | Games roll up into a season. Fall 2026 pays $10,000 to the top 10 after the Singapore Major ($5,000 / $2,000 / $1,000 / $300 for 4th–10th), plus sponsor skin prizes. | [V] same source |
| Seasonal vs non-seasonal | Some games don't count toward the season. BLAST Bounty's online part was non-seasonal; the seasonal game started at the LAN quarter-finals. | [V] [HLTV, BLAST Bounty](https://www.hltv.org/news/45158/blast-bounty-teams-format-schedule-prizes-talent-fantasy) |
| Season points | Season standings award points by percentile finish in each game. One update gave 18 points to everyone finishing between the top 1% and top 5%. | [S] [HLTV Fall Season update (2023)](https://www.hltv.org/news/36599/hltv-fantasy-updated-for-fall-season-notifications-roles-boosters-and-more) |
| Roster | Five players, $1,000,000 budget, at most two from one team. | [S] [skin.club guide](https://community.skin.club/en/news/how-to-win-hltv-fantasy-league-with-james-banks) |
| Prices | Historically based on LAN rating over the past six months, adjusted for team ranking. Recent price changes follow rating and team rank. New players with no data get a flat price ($180k for unranked teams). A debut player (donk) was priced by community vote ($260k). | [S] [Fantasy Scout](https://www.hltv.org/news/29095/fantasy-scout-iem-katowice); [V] [karrigan price](https://www.hltv.org/news/41231/karrigan-goes-for-177k-in-blast-lisbon-fantasy), [donk vote](https://www.hltv.org/news/38260/europe-rmr-b-fantasy-about-to-start-with-donk-priced-at-260k) |
| Price range | Stars sit around $200k–$260k (donk $250k at EPL S24, s1mple $200k, karrigan $177k). | [V] [donk EPL S24](https://www.hltv.org/news/45606/donk-is-second-most-picked-in-epl-s24-group-stage-fantasy-despite-250k-price), [s1mple](https://www.hltv.org/news/41717/s1mple-goes-for-200k-and-new-booster-set-to-debut-in-iem-dallas-groups-fantasy) |
| Scoring | Four parts added together: rating points, team points, role points, booster points. | [S] [IEM Rio tips](https://www.hltv.org/news/34907/fantasy-tips-and-tricks-for-iem-rio) |
| Rating points | Half the gap between the player's HLTV rating and 1.00, in hundredths: a 1.12 rating gives +6, a 0.80 gives −10. | [S] same source |
| Team points | +6 for a win, −3 for a loss. The source doesn't say whether this is per map or per match; check in the hands-on pass. | [S] same source |
| Format adjustment | BO1 points halved; BO5 points multiplied by 1.25. | [V] [Fall Season update](https://www.hltv.org/news/36599/hltv-fantasy-updated-for-fall-season-notifications-roles-boosters-and-more) (2023, may have changed) |
| Roles | You assign each player a role for the whole event (e.g. Leader, Support, Main AWP, Entry Fragger, Multifragger, HS Machine, Camper, Stathunter). Each scores +5, +2 or −2 depending on role-specific thresholds. Roles were rebalanced in 2023 and again later. | [S] same and [IEM Rio tips](https://www.hltv.org/news/34907/fantasy-tips-and-tricks-for-iem-rio) |
| Boosters | One-match bonuses (+5 if the condition triggers). Regularly retuned: eased ~20% after the MR12 switch; new ones added such as "Chicken Out" (forces a player's score to 0) and "Skin in the game" (doubles team points: +6 win, −3 loss). | [V] [IEM Sydney](https://www.hltv.org/news/37245/boosters-updated-ahead-of-iem-sydney-group-stage-fantasy-game), [EPL](https://www.hltv.org/news/39764/new-fantasy-booster-starts-with-epl-groups-a-and-b), [IEM Dallas](https://www.hltv.org/news/41717/s1mple-goes-for-200k-and-new-booster-set-to-debut-in-iem-dallas-groups-fantasy) |
| Notifications | Added in the 2023 Fall update. Detail not available. | [S] same source |
| Ownership | HLTV publishes "most-picked" players as news stories (e.g. karrigan most-picked at PGL Astana). | [V] [PGL Astana](https://www.hltv.org/news/44530/karrigan-the-most-picked-fantasy-player-as-pgl-astana-about-to-start) |
| Private leagues | Free, playable in official leagues and in private or public leagues run by others. Communities like Dust2.us run Discord leagues per Major stage. | [S] [Spring season (2021)](https://www.hltv.org/news/33106/fantasy-returns-with-10000-spring-season), [Dust2.us](https://dust2.us/news/47005/compete-in-dust2s-iem-chengdu-fantasy-league) |
| Sponsors | Betting and skin sites (22BET, Hellcase, LOOT.BET). | [V] Fall update; EWC article; [LOOT.BET](https://www.hltv.org/news/29560/lootbet-season-6-playoffs-fantasy-goes-live-with-prizes) |
| Rating 3.0 | HLTV's rating, which drives prices and points, changed to 3.0 in August 2025 (eco-adjusted kills, "Round Swing") and was revised again in October 2025 after criticism (Round Swing reduced from 40% to 33%). | [S] [Escorenews](https://escorenews.com/en/csgo/article/71475-how-hltv-rating-3-0-formula-actually-works-round-swing-and-eco-adjustment-explained), [Dust2.us](https://www.dust2.us/news/67183/hltv-releases-update-for-rating-30) |

[Not examined]: the contest list, team builder, mobile layout, player pages, leaderboard UI, private-league creation flow and notification behaviour. Points 1–4, 10, 11, 13 and 15 of the brief's HLTV list need the hands-on pass.

### Assessment

**Exceptional:**
- **Event-native.** Every notable CS event gets a game, announced with news coverage ("donk priced at $260k"). Fantasy becomes part of the editorial calendar, not a separate product. [V]
- **Real strategic tension.** The $1M cap plus the two-per-team limit forces you to spread picks across the bracket. Strategy guides talk about "stacking reliable silver-tier picks" and splitting rosters across bracket halves. [S]
- **The season wrapper.** Percentile-based season points let one person's results across many events add up, and reward consistency without punishing small fields. [S]
- **Price stories are content.** Price reveals and most-picked lists double as articles. [V]

**Friction:**
- **Roles and boosters stack up.** A new player must learn roles (with thresholds), boosters (one-use, per match) and rating maths before their first pick. HLTV retunes them constantly (MR12, a 2023 overhaul, new boosters most seasons), which itself shows they're hard to balance. [I, from V]
- **Scoring rests on a black box.** Rating points come from HLTV Rating, which HLTV doesn't publish in full and which changed twice in 2025, criticised by pros and analysts. A fan can't check their own score by hand. [S] [ggscore](https://ggscore.com/en/csgo/news/29773)
- **Format unfairness had to be patched.** Halving BO1 and boosting BO5 is a blunt fix and adds one more rule to learn. [I]
- **Fragmented games.** Group stage and playoffs are often separate games, so the "my team over the whole tournament" story breaks up. [I, from V]
- **Gambling-adjacent sponsors.** This sets the tone of the product and limits partnerships with publishers, which ban such associations in some cases. [I]

### What to adapt, avoid and differentiate

| | |
|---|---|
| **Adapt** | $1M cap, 5 players, max 2 per team [R]. Price reveal and most-picked stories as shareable content [R]. A season built on percentile finishes, not raw points [R]. One game per event with clear lock times [R]. |
| **Avoid** | Roles with hidden thresholds at launch [R]. Proprietary-rating-based scoring [R]. Many separate games per event [R]. Betting and skin-gambling sponsors [R]. |
| **Differentiate** | Transparent scoring anyone can recompute [R]. One tournament-long entry with stage transfers [R]. Social leagues and one entry in many boards [R]. Modern, mobile-first builder [R]. |
| **Study in hands-on pass** | Team builder, player search and filters, contest list, leaderboard and season pages, private-league join, mobile web. |
| **Applicability** | Very high. HLTV defines what CS fans expect fantasy to be. |

## 2. Sleeper

**Why it matters:** Sleeper is the UX and social benchmark for modern fantasy, and it ran an official esports fantasy product with Riot.

### What we know

| Area | Finding | Label |
|------|---------|-------|
| Positioning | Started in 2018 as an ad-free, social-first fantasy football host. Over 5 million people use its fantasy football leagues. Grows through invites rather than paid acquisition. | [S] [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/sleeper); [V] Sleeper job post via [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs/3130024-user-researcher-ux), [BetaList](https://betalist.com/startups/sleeper) |
| Chat | Almost every screen has league chat embedded; GIFs and images supported. Reviewers single it out. | [S] [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/05/12/fantasy-sports-startup-sleeper-closes-series-b-led-by-a16z-as-it-expands-to-esports-amid-pandemic), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleeper-sports/id987367543) |
| Commissioner tools | Thorough, but critics want deeper controls and more scoring formats; peak-time outages noted. Can't set positional minimums or maximums. | [S] [unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/sleeper-espn-yahoo-nfl-cbs-fantasy-sports-apps-ranked-2026), [commissioner guide](https://tiny.write.as/supercommish/introduction-to-sleeper) |
| Drafting | Mock drafts against ADP bots when the room isn't full; separate redraft and dynasty ADP; mocks saved for review. In-draft pick trading. Per-pick timers, auto-pick on a missed turn, in-draft chat (Premier League product). | [S] [FantasyPros (2021)](https://www.fantasypros.com/2021/08/sleeper-play-fantasy-football-together/amp/), [AllAboutFPL](https://allaboutfpl.com/2024/09/5-reasons-to-choose-sleeper-app-for-your-draft-fantasy-leagues/) |
| Fantasy LCS / LoL | Relaunched with Riot for 2021 Spring. Up to 10 teams per league; draft 5 players; weekly head-to-head; playoffs for the best records. | [V] [Sleeper Fantasy LCS](https://sleeper.com/fantasy-lcs); [S] [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/fantasy/lcs/) |
| Pick/ban | Each week, pick one champion per owned player for bonus points (double that champion's points, per Sleeper's own write-up), and ban one champion per opposing player to cancel those points. Default pick bonus 1.5 and ban penalty 0.5 in 2021. | [V] [Sleeper blog](https://sleeper.com/blog/deep-dive-into-the-gameplay-of-sleepers-fantasy-league-of-legends/); [S] [NerdStreet](https://nerdstreet.com/news/2022/2/prep-for-fantasy-league-of-legends-lcs-draft-on-sleeper) |
| Draft formats | Snake, linear and third-round reversal. | [V] [Sleeper Fantasy LCS](https://sleeper.com/fantasy-lcs) |
| LoL scoring (2021 beta) | Kill +2, death −0.5, assist +1.5, CS 0.01; triple +2, quadra +5, penta +10; 10+ kills or assists +2. Team: first blood +2, dragon +1, baron +2, tower +1, win +2. | [S] [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/news/league-of-legends-to-offer-official-fantasy-lcs-beta/) |
| Data | Sleeper's esports fantasy used PandaScore for history and live updates, including first blood, first tower and first drake. | [V] [PandaScore case study](https://www.pandascore.co/blog/how-sleeper-uses-pandascore-for-esports-fantasy) |
| Status today | Last confirmed season: Summer 2024. For 2026 Riot restored a standalone LCS and is turning its own LCS Fantasy app into a hub. I found nothing confirming Sleeper still runs LoL fantasy. | [V] [LCS/LTA X post (2024)](https://x.com/LCSOfficial/status/1800325093312311526), [LCS 2026 Address](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/lcs-2026-address) |

[Not examined]: league creation and invite flow, draft room, player cards, live matchup view, standings, profile, mobile information layout.

### Assessment

**Exceptional:**
- **Social as the core.** Chat is everywhere, not a tab. Sleeper grew mostly on invites. [S]
- **Draft experience.** Bots fill empty seats, mocks are kept, timers and auto-pick handle absent managers. [S]
- **Esports-native mechanic.** Champion pick/ban created a weekly decision tied to the real game's draft, which no stats-only fantasy has. [V]

**Friction:**
- **Head-to-head weekly leagues need a long season.** Esports tournaments last 1–4 weeks. Sleeper's LoL product only worked on regional leagues with 8–10 week splits. [I]
- **Pick/ban is LoL-specific and adds weekly homework.** Good for draft leagues of committed friends, too much for casual tournament fans. [I]
- **The esports product depended on a publisher partnership.** When Riot's league structure changed (LTA, then back to LCS), the product's future became unclear. [I, from V]

### What to adapt, avoid and differentiate

| | |
|---|---|
| **Adapt** | League chat attached to the leaderboard [R]. Invite-link-first growth [R]. Bot-filled mock drafts and auto-pick for Mode B [R]. Multiple draft orders [R]. |
| **Avoid** | Weekly head-to-head as the core of a short tournament [R]. Pick/ban in the flagship mode [R]. |
| **Differentiate** | Bring Sleeper-quality social to tournament contests, which Sleeper never did [R]. |
| **Study in hands-on pass** | Create league → invite → draft room → live matchup → league chat on mobile. |
| **Applicability** | High for UX and social; low for game format. |

## 3. DraftKings daily fantasy

**Why it matters:** the reference for salary-cap lineup building, contest lobbies and captain mechanics. We study product architecture only, not wagering.

### What we know

| Area | Finding | Label |
|------|---------|-------|
| CS2 format | 6 players, $50,000 cap, no positions. Captain scores 1.5x and costs more salary. Points from kills, assists and deaths plus bonuses for clutches, entry kills, quad kills and aces. "Scoring is adjusted based on the length of the game." | [S] [RotoWire CS2 guide](https://www.rotowire.com/esports/article/dfs-101-how-to-play-csgo-on-draftkings-50216), [RotoWire optimizer](https://www.rotowire.com/daily/esports/optimizer.php?competitionID=5) |
| LoL format | 6 players (Top, Jungle, Mid, ADC, Support, plus captain) and 1 Team slot, $50,000 cap. Captain 1.5x. +20 points per player for a 2-0 sweep in a BO3, in place of the unplayed game. Kills are the most valuable stat (3). Typical stack: 4 from one team, 3 from another. | [S] [RotoWire LoL guide](https://www.rotowire.com/esports/article/dfs-101-how-to-play-league-of-legends-on-draftkings-50118) |
| Late swap | Players lock when their own game starts; you can edit unlocked spots. DraftKings has run contests with late swap off, and once removed it for NBA "so customers can enjoy the games". | [S] [Stokastic](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/dfs-strategy/dfs-late-swap), [RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/articles/draftkings-drops-late-swap-for-nba-1477088) |
| Current esports status | Undetermined. One undated page says CS2 contests are offered; I found no 2026 confirmation. | [S] [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/fantasy/draftkings-csgo) |

[Not examined]: lobby, filters, player modal, remaining-salary bar, ownership display, lineup review, live scoring. The official [how-to-play page](https://www.draftkings.com/how-to-play) was listed in search but couldn't be opened.

### Assessment

**Exceptional:**
- **The captain mechanic.** One decision, 1.5x points, priced higher. Easy to explain, strategically meaningful, and it creates "who's your captain?" conversation. [S]
- **Explicit series-length fixes.** The LoL sweep bonus replaces points from a game that wasn't needed, which is a principled answer to the BO3 problem. [S]
- **Mature lineup-building conventions.** Remaining salary, average remaining per slot, lineup validation and ownership are expected by experienced players. [I]

**Friction:**
- **Built for many contests per day,** with heavy research expected. Optimisers and third-party tools are part of the competitive meta, which disadvantages casual fans. [I, from S]
- **Paid entry and wagering framing.** Out of scope for us and legally heavy. [I]
- **Six-player rosters with a separate captain slot** add pricing complexity (two prices per player). [I]

### What to adapt, avoid and differentiate

| | |
|---|---|
| **Adapt** | Captain at 1.5x [R]. A sticky remaining-salary bar with per-slot average [R]. Lineup validation that explains what's wrong [R]. Ownership % after lock [R]. A sweep-style correction for series length [R]. |
| **Avoid** | Per-player late swap at launch (confusing in multi-day events) [R]. Two prices per player [R]. Optimiser-driven multi-entry [R]. |
| **Differentiate** | Event-long contests instead of daily slates [R]. Ownership and projections that explain, not just list [R]. |
| **Study in hands-on pass** | Lineup builder on mobile web, player card modal, contest detail and rules, live leaderboard. |
| **Applicability** | High for the builder; low for contest economics. |

## 4. KBOOM

**Why it matters:** a current, free, multi-game (LoL and VALORANT) fantasy product with collectible cards.

### What we know

| Area | Finding | Label |
|------|---------|-------|
| Coverage | LEC, LCK, LPL, LCS, LFL and VCT. Expanded to Tier-1 LoL leagues in summer 2026. Prize pool reported (€1,500). | [V] [KBOOM](https://app.kboom.gg/); [S] [Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/all/articles/kboom-brings-fantasy-league-to-league-of-legends-tier-1/en) |
| Lineup | 5 players plus 1 coach. In LoL, one player per role. | [V] [KBOOM FAQ](https://app.kboom.gg/frequently-asked-questions/) |
| Cards | Open packs to collect player and coach cards. Rarity sets a score multiplier: Common 1x, Uncommon 1.01–1.25x, Rare 1.26–1.50x, Epic 1.51–1.75x, Legendary 1.76–2x. You can field a player without a card, but with no multiplier. | [V] same |
| Coaches | No multiplier; each coach type adds a perk (e.g. bonus for objectives or series wins). | [V] same |
| Packs | Starter packs on joining; free packs every 12 hours per active league; premium packs for Gems; duplicates "burn" into Gems. Cards last one league season. | [V] same |
| Predictions | Separate mode; backing the side the community doubts earns more points. | [V] [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bloometa.kboom&hl=en_US) |
| Leaderboards | Per league, with season-end rewards. | [V] FAQ; [GitBook](https://kboom.gitbook.io/how-to-use-kboom/fantasy-league/leaderboard-and-competition) |

[Not examined]: the app itself, card presentation, onboarding, whether Gems can be bought with money (likely, given "premium" packs, but unconfirmed).

### Assessment

**Exceptional:**
- **A reason to open the app every 12 hours** (free packs) even between matches. [V]
- **Contrarian prediction scoring** makes predictions skill-expressive rather than a popularity vote. [V]
- **Coaches as perk slots** add flavour without new stats. [V]

**Friction and the cards question:**
- **Multipliers up to 2x from rarity mean the same lineup can score double for one user.** Luck in pack opening, or purchase if Gems are bought, directly changes competitive results. That's pay-to-win or luck-to-win in a skill game. [I, from V]
- **Packs and burning add an economy to learn** before the fantasy game itself. [I]
- **Legal and policy risk.** Random packs bought with money resemble loot boxes, which are regulated or banned in some countries. [I]

**Verdict on collectible cards:** they add retention, but distort fair competition. **Don't use cards that affect scores. Cosmetic-only cards are fine.** [R]

### What to adapt, avoid and differentiate

| | |
|---|---|
| **Adapt** | Contrarian bonus in Pick'Ems (e.g. more points for correctly picking a team under 30% of picks) [R]. A light "coach" or "team" slot idea for LoL later [R]. Multi-league architecture [R]. |
| **Avoid** | Score multipliers from cards or purchases [R]. Packs bought with money [R]. |
| **Differentiate** | Fair, skill-only competition as an explicit promise [R]. |
| **Applicability** | Medium: useful for multi-game architecture and retention ideas; card mechanics rejected. |

## 5. Riot Pick'Ems and Riot's LCS Fantasy

**Why it matters:** Riot's official products set expectations for LoL and VALORANT fans. During research I found a direct competitor not in the brief: **Riot's own LCS Fantasy**.

### Worlds Pick'Em (LoL) [S] (2025 event)

- **Two phases:** Swiss-stage picks (which eight teams reach knockouts, locked before Swiss), then knockout picks reopen after Swiss ends. Playable in the League client and on the web. [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/how-to-play-worlds-2025-pickem), [Strafe](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/worlds-2025-pick-ems-guide-and-rewards-swiss-stage-and-crystall-ball/)
- **Scoring:** 10 points per correct qualifying team, 15 for the scoreline picks (3-0 / 0-3); extra for correct 3-0, 3-1, 3-2 records.
- **Rewards are real in-game items:** a participation emote, emotes for the top 50% and top 25%, a skin for the top 5,000, seven skins for a perfect score. [Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/news/worlds-2025-pickems)

### VALORANT Champions Pick'Em [S] (2025 event)

- **Group stage is advance/fail:** choose which 2 teams in each group advance. Separate playoff window. [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/guides/valorant/valorant-champions-2025-pickems/), [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/valorant/news/vct-champions-2025-pickems-guide)
- **Rewards:** a spray for making one pick; gun buddies for the top 50% and 20%; a title for a perfect score (440 points; nobody achieved it).
- **Factions:** influencer-led groups with shared averages (only the top 75% of each faction counts). [Pick'Ems wiki](https://valorant.fandom.com/wiki/Pick'Ems)

### LCS Fantasy (Riot, 2026) [V]

From the [official rules](https://lcsofficial.gg/en/rules):

- **Free, sign in with Riot ID.** 100-gold budget. Rosters lock before the first game of each week.
- **Scores are averaged per game across a weekend, "to account for the variable number of games in a series".** This directly validates our averaging approach.
- **Champion bonus multipliers;** only the highest applies.
- **A coach slot** scoring the average of that coach's team.
- **On sign-up you pick a favourite team and are automatically entered into the LCS league and that team's league. "You can enter as many leaderboards as you want"**, including friend and streamer leagues. This validates one entry in many leaderboards.
- **Riot is turning the app into an LCS hub** (schedules, standings, content, fantasy) with achievements. [V] [LCS 2026 Address](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/lcs-2026-address)

### Assessment

**Exceptional:**
- **Zero setup.** Pick'Em sits inside the game client players already open. [S]
- **Rewards with real value** (skins, titles) and percentile tiers, so most participants "win" something. [S]
- **Two-phase predictions** match how fans think: commit early, then re-engage when the bracket is set. [S]
- **LCS Fantasy's design choices** (per-game averaging, a favourite-team league, unlimited leaderboards) are close to what we'd propose. [V]

**Friction:**
- **Pick'Em ends when the event ends;** there's no lasting identity across events beyond rewards. [I]
- **Multi-phase windows are easy to miss.** Coverage cites conflicting dates between outlets. [S]

**What this means for us:**
- **For LoL and VALORANT, we can't beat Riot on Pick'Ems.** Riot's are free, in-client and pay in skins. [I]
- **LCS Fantasy covers NA LoL fantasy officially.** [V]
- **Our LoL/VALORANT value must come from cross-game identity, social leagues, international events (Worlds, Masters, Champions) and better fantasy depth,** not from Pick'Ems. [R]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Adapt** | Percentile reward tiers so most players get something [R]. Two-phase picks (before the event, after groups) [R]. Per-game averaging [R]. Favourite-team auto-league [R]. |
| **Avoid** | Competing head-on with Riot Pick'Ems for LoL/VALORANT [R]. Factions at launch [R]. |
| **Applicability** | High for mechanics; medium-low for market entry in LoL and VALORANT. |

## 6. Pickstop and Valve's Major Pick'Em

### Pickstop [V] (limited)

- **Positioning:** "Pick your winning teams, compete against your friends, and earn trophies for your global ranking." [Pickstop](https://pickstop.gg/)
- **Active in CS2:** picks open for StarSeries Fall 2026, BLAST playoffs and other events. [PickstopCS on X](https://twitter.com/PickstopCS)
- **[Not examined]:** rules, scoring, landing pages, bracket interaction, profiles, sharing.
- **Don't confuse it with PickR (pickr.gg),** a separate free esports prediction game with private leagues. [S]

### Valve's Major Pick'Em [S]

- **Picks per Swiss stage:** two 3-0, six "advance" (3-1 or 3-2 only) and two 0-3. A 3-0 team doesn't count in an advance slot. [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/cs2-major-pick-em-guide/), [Dust2.us](https://www.dust2.us/news/45867/valve-releases-copenhagen-major-viewer-pass-with-new-pickem-challenge)
- **Requires the paid Viewer Pass** and upgrades an in-game coin. [Fantha Tracks](https://www.fanthatracks.com/blogs/cs2-pickem-explained-how-does-it-works/)

### Assessment

- **CS2 Pick'Ems has a real gap.** Valve's version is paywalled and in-client, with no social leagues; third-party sites like Pickstop fill it. [I]
- **A free, social, well-designed CS2 Pick'Em tied to our fantasy contests is a credible acquisition funnel.** [R]
- **Learn from Valve's slot design.** Distinct 3-0, advance and 0-3 slots make a simple prediction strategic. [R]

| **Avoid** | Paywalls on predictions [R]. Asking for Steam authentication codes (Steam warns about third-party sites doing so) [S] [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/730/discussions/0/143388408895012637). |

## 7. Underdog Best Ball

[S] From Underdog's [contest guidelines](https://help.underdogsports.com/en/articles/8991638-best-ball-contest-guidelines) (as indexed) and guides.

- **No in-season management:** you draft, and the best lineup is used automatically each week.
- **Two draft speeds:** fast (about 20–30 seconds per pick) and slow (hours per pick). Slow drafts pause overnight, and the pause grows as the season nears. Timers speed up to 60 seconds two hours before the first game.
- **Tournament advancement:** in Best Ball Mania 5, the top 2 of each 12-team league advanced to quarter-finals in 13-team groups, then semi-finals in 16-team groups, then a final.
- **Sit & Go contests** run alongside the big tournaments. [Underdog](https://help.underdogfantasy.com/en/articles/10716487-best-ball-sit-n-go)

### Assessment

**Exceptional:**
- **"Draft and done" removes the main reason casual players quit fantasy:** forgetting to manage the team. [I]
- **Two draft speeds** serve both groups: fast for people who want it now, slow for friends across time zones. [I]
- **Overnight pauses** are thoughtful: nobody gets auto-picked while asleep. [S]

**Friction:**
- **Best ball needs depth (benches) and many scoring periods,** neither of which esports tournaments have. A 5-player roster in a 2-week event has nothing to "best ball". [I]
- **Multi-entry and advancement structures are built for paid contests at scale.** [I]

**Principle to adapt:** don't punish absence. Our version is **Autopilot**, which automatically replaces eliminated players at stage windows for users who don't return (see [scoring doc](04-gameplay-and-scoring.md#autopilot)). Also adopt slow drafts with overnight pauses for Mode B. [R]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Adapt** | Autopilot replacements [R]. Fast and slow drafts with quiet hours for Mode B [R]. |
| **Avoid** | Pure best-ball for tournament fantasy [R]. Advancement-pyramid contests [R]. |
| **Applicability** | Medium: principles, not formats. |

## 9. Lessons from failed fantasy esports products

- **AlphaDraft** (FanDuel's esports daily fantasy) shut down, citing "the current eSports landscape". [S] [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/alphadraft-closure-fantasy-esports)
- **Vulcun** ended paid contests, citing the US legal landscape. [S] [Gaming Intelligence](https://www.gamingintelligence.com/markets/28011-vulcun-to-shut-down-real-money-paid-fantasy-esports-contests/)
- **Commentators pointed to a small market** facing the same legal exposure as mainstream daily fantasy. [S]
- **Sleeper's esports product depended on Riot's league structure,** which then changed. [I]

**Lesson [R]:**
- **Stay free-to-play** with no paid entry.
- **Keep costs small** relative to a niche but passionate audience.
- **Don't depend on one publisher partnership** for the core product.
- **Win on community and distribution,** not on prize money.

## 10. Cross-cutting findings by lens

### A. First-time experience

- **The best products need zero setup.** Riot Pick'Em is in-client; LCS Fantasy signs in with Riot ID and auto-enrols you in a favourite-team league. [V]
- **HLTV asks for the most learning before value:** roles, boosters, rating maths. [I]
- **[R] Our target: value before sign-up.** Build a lineup without an account; sign in only to save it. Explain the captain and the cap inline at the moment they matter.

### B. Information architecture

- **Event-first products organise around the tournament** (HLTV, Riot Pick'Em). League-first products organise around your league (Sleeper). [I]
- **[R] Ours is event-first, with leagues as views onto entries,** never separate games.

### C. Gameplay

- **Proven building blocks:** a salary cap, a per-team limit and a captain. [S/V]
- **Series-length fairness is a known problem everyone patches:** HLTV halves BO1, DraftKings adds a sweep bonus, LCS Fantasy averages per game. [V/S]

### D. Live engagement

- **[Not examined] for every product.** No evidence on refresh rates or how corrections are handled.
- **[R] We must define our own:** provisional scores after each map, final settlement later, and a visible audit trail per player.

### E. Social

- **Proven social mechanics:** Sleeper's chat and invites; LCS Fantasy's unlimited leaderboards; VALORANT's influencer factions; Discord-run HLTV leagues. [S/V]
- **[R] Creator leagues and one-entry-many-boards are the growth engine.**

### F. Monetization

- **HLTV:** free, funded by betting and skin sponsors. [V]
- **KBOOM:** premium packs. [V]
- **Riot:** a cost centre funded by the game. [I]
- **Sleeper:** venture-funded, later added paid products. [S]
- **[R] For us:** free and fair, with revenue from sponsors, creator tools and cosmetics. See [business model](07-roadmap-business-retention.md#business-model).
