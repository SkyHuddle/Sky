## Lessons

1. The strongest brands are short real words with a fantasy meaning (Sleeper, Underdog). Literal "Draft / Pick / Fantasy / Esports" compounds are overused and mostly in the graveyard.
2. Keep "fantasy" and "pick'em" for the subtitle: "Name: Esports Fantasy & Pick'Em".
3. Avoid betting-coded words (bet, odds, stake, edge, lock, market) and misspellings (Betr, PickR). This counts against the current name, Lockin.
4. The name must work for every game: CS2, Call of Duty (4v4), VALORANT and League of Legends. Avoid team sizes and slang from one game.

## Recommendation

1. **Seeded**: the cleanest result. It's tournament language that fits brackets and pick'em, and a seeded player is a ranked one.
2. **Upset**: clean, and the best moment in pick'em. It is close in spirit to Underdog, and it's a known LoL pro's handle.
3. **Decider**: the deciding map in any best-of series. Decider.com is a large media brand in another category, so the .com is not available.
