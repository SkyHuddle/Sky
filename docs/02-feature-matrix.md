## What the matrix shows

1. **No product combines a fair salary-cap game, tournament-long contests, social leagues and a cross-event identity.** HLTV has the first two, Riot's LCS Fantasy the social model, Pickstop the identity. That combination is our opening. [I]
2. **In CS2, the only serious fantasy competitor is HLTV.** DraftKings' current CS2 status is unclear, and its format is paid daily fantasy anyway. [I]
3. **In LoL and VALORANT, Riot's official products and KBOOM already cover the basics.** We would enter later and need a cross-game reason to switch. [I]
